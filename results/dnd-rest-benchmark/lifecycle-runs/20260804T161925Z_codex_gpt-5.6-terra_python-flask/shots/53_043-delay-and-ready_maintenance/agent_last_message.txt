Implemented delay and ready turn support in `app.py`.

- Delay accepts `{"new_index": …}`, persists the reordered initiative list, keeps the delayed actor current at the new index, and returns the new order.
- Ready accepts a nonblank trigger, requires the current combatant, and leaves turn order unchanged.
- `run.sh` remains foreground-ready and passed shell validation.

Verified with Flask test-client flow plus `py_compile`; no server was started.
