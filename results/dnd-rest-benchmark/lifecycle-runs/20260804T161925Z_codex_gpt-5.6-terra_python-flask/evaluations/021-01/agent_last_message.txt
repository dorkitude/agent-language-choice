Implemented GM narration in `app.py`.

- Adds `POST /v1/play/campaigns/<id>/narrations`
- Enforces authenticated DM ownership; players receive 403
- Persists append-only narration events per campaign
- Returns 201 with `sequence`, `kind`, `actor`, and `text`
- Sequences begin at 1 and increment transactionally

Verified syntax and in-process Flask checks. `run.sh` remains executable and starts the foreground server as required.
