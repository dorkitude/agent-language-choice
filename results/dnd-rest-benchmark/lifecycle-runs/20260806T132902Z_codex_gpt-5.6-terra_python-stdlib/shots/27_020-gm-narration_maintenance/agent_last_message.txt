Implemented GM narration in `server.py`.

- `POST /v1/play/campaigns/{id}/narrations`
- DM owner only; players receive 403
- Returns ordered append-only events starting at sequence 1 per campaign
- Narrations persist in SQLite and reset with storage
- `run.sh` remains executable and foreground-ready

Validated with `python3 -m py_compile server.py`. No server was started.
