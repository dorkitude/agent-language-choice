Implemented campaign start support.

- Added DM-owner-only `POST /v1/play/campaigns/{id}/start`.
- Requires at least two party members; activates once with deterministic first actor `player-a`.
- Returns 403 for players/non-owner DMs and 409 for under-populated or active campaigns.
- Prevents joining after activation.
- Updated reset/schema handling; `run.sh` remains foreground, `PORT`-based, and executable.

Validated PHP and shell syntax plus all new success/error paths through CGI without starting the server.
