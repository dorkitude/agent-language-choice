Implemented party membership support in `app.rb`.

- Added `POST /v1/play/campaigns/:id/members`
- Requires an authenticated player; DMs receive 403
- Returns 201 with `username`, `character_id`, `name`, and `class`
- Enforces one membership per player per campaign, globally unique character IDs, lobby-only joining, and party capacity with 409 conflicts
- Memberships persist in SQLite and are cleared by storage reset
- `run.sh` remains ready and binds Puma to `127.0.0.1:$PORT`

Verified syntax with `ruby -c app.rb` and `bash -n run.sh`.
