Implemented Player Combat Actions.

- Added `POST /v1/play/campaigns/:id/encounters/:enc_id/actions`.
- Supports `attack`, `help`, `dodge`, and `ready`.
- Records `combat_action` events with `type`, `target`, and `text`.
- Enforces that only the active player combatant may act (409 otherwise).
- Does not advance combat or campaign turns.
- Preserves target data in recent event payloads.

Verified Ruby syntax, route dispatch, and `run.sh` syntax without starting the server.
