Implemented GM narration support.

- Added `POST /v1/play/campaigns/:id/narrations`
- DM campaign owner can append narration events (201)
- Player attempts receive 403
- Events return `sequence`, `kind: "narration"`, `actor: "dm"`, and `text`
- Sequence is append-only and starts at 1 per campaign
- Added persistent event storage and reset cleanup

Verified `app.rb` syntax and the narration route; `run.sh` remains executable and ready.
