Implemented the combat-to-exploration transition.

- Added `POST /v1/play/campaigns/:id/encounters/:enc_id/end`.
- It requires the campaign owner, closes an active combat encounter, restores the paused actor, and returns phase `"exploration"`.
- Returns 409 when the campaign is not in combat.
- Encounter creation now records the paused actor and marks the campaign `"combat"`.

Verified syntax, `run.sh`, and the full transition flow in-process without starting the server.
