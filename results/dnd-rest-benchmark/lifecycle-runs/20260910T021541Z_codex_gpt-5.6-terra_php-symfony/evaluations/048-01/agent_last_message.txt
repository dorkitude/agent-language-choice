Implemented level progression.

- Added owner-only `POST /v1/play/campaigns/{id}/characters/{char_id}/level-up`.
- Persists character level and Constitution modifier with backward-compatible defaults.
- Uses deterministic rounded-up average hit-die gains; rogues use `1d8` (+5) plus Constitution modifier.
- Enforces exactly one level at a time and returns 400/403 for invalid/non-owner requests.
- `run.sh` remains executable and foreground-bound to `127.0.0.1:$PORT`.

Verified with `php -l index.php`, `php -l storage.php`, and `bash -n run.sh`.
