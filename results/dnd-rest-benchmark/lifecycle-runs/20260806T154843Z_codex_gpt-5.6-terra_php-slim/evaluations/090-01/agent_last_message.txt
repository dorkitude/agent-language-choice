Implemented backup/restore support in `index.php`.

- Owner-only create, list, and restore endpoints.
- Immutable sequential `backup-N` snapshots of story/status.
- Restore preserves DM notes and does not create events or backups.
- Backup storage participates in reset cleanup.
- `run.sh` remains executable and foreground-launches Slim on `127.0.0.1:$PORT`.

Verified with `php -l index.php`, `bash -n run.sh`, and static route checks. Server was not started.
