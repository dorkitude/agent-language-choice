Implemented NPCs and factions in [src/main.rs](src/main.rs):

- `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/factions`
- `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/npcs`
- `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/relationships`

Data is persisted in SQLite, reset is supported, and invalid/duplicate/missing resources return non-2xx responses. NPCs require an existing faction in the same campaign.

Verified with `cargo test` (13 passing) and a direct Rust 2024 compilation. `run.sh` remains ready and was not started.
