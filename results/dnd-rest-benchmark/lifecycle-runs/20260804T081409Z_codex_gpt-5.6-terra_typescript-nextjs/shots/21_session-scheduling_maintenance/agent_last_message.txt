Implemented campaign session scheduling and attendance endpoints with SQLite persistence:

- `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/sessions`
- `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/sessions/{session_id}/attendance`
- `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/sessions/next`

Sessions persist across restarts and are included in storage reset. Validation, unknown-resource handling, duplicate prevention, and chronological next-session lookup are included.

`./run.sh` remains executable and serves Next.js on `127.0.0.1:$PORT`.

Verified with `./node_modules/.bin/tsc --noEmit`. I did not start the server.
