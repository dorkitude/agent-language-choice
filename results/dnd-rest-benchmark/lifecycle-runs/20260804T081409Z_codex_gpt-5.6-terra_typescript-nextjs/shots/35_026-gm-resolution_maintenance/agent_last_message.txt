Implemented GM resolution support.

- Added `POST /v1/play/campaigns/[id]/resolutions`.
- Only the campaign owner can resolve while it is their turn.
- Player resolution attempts return 409.
- Resolution events advance play to player B (then round-robin), returning `next_actor` and incremented `turn_number`.
- Turn reads now reflect resolution-driven actor and turn number.

Verified with `npx tsc --noEmit`. `run.sh` remains ready and starts Next.js on `127.0.0.1:$PORT` in the foreground.
