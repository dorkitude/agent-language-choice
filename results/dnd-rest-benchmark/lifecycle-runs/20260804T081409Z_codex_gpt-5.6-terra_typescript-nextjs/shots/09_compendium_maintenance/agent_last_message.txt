Implemented SQLite-backed compendium APIs:

- `POST /v1/compendium/monsters`
- `GET /v1/compendium/monsters/[slug]`
- `POST /v1/compendium/items`
- `GET /v1/compendium/items/[slug]`

Records persist in `game.db`, duplicate slugs return `409`, unknown records return `404`, and storage reset clears compendium data too.

Verified with `tsc --noEmit`. `run.sh` remains ready and starts Next.js on `127.0.0.1:$PORT` in the foreground.
