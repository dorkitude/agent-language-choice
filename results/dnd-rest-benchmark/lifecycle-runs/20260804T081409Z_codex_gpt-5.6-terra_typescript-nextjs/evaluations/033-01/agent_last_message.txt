Implemented Travel Turns.

- Added `POST /v1/play/campaigns/{id}/turn/travel`.
- Validates the active player and an outbound edge from the current scene location (or first location fallback).
- Records a `travel` event, returns `201`, and advances turn to DM.
- Invalid destinations and out-of-turn attempts return `409`.
- Travel leaves the location graph and current scene unchanged.

Verified with `tsc --noEmit`; `run.sh` remains executable and ready.
