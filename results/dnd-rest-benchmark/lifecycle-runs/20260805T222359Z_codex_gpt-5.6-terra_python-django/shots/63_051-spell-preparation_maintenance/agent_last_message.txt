Implemented spell preparation endpoints and persistent state.

- `PUT` validates owner, caster class, known spells, and level limit.
- `GET` allows campaign members and always returns `prepared_spells`, including `[]`.
- Level-1 wizard maximum is 1; prepared order is preserved.
- `run.sh` remains ready for foreground startup on `127.0.0.1:$PORT`.

Verified with Django checks, bytecode compilation, shell syntax, and direct Django-client endpoint tests.
