Implemented campaign analytics reporting in `main.go`:

- `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/analytics/summary`
- `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/analytics/risk-report`

The handlers aggregate persisted campaign state deterministically, including quest, NPC, session, inventory, character, and DM signals. `run.sh` remains executable and starts the server in the foreground.

Verified with `go test ./...` using a writable temporary Go cache.
