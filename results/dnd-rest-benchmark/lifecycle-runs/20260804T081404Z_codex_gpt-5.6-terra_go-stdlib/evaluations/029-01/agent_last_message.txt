Implemented the durable campaign document feature in `main.go`.

- Owner can `PUT /v1/play/campaigns/{id}/document` and receives `story` plus `dm_notes`.
- Campaign members with player role can `GET` only `story`; private notes are never serialized.
- Player updates are rejected with 403.
- Document storage survives server restarts and is reset with storage reset.
- `run.sh` remains executable and starts the foreground server correctly.

Verified with `GOCACHE=/private/tmp/dndrest-go-cache go test ./...`.
