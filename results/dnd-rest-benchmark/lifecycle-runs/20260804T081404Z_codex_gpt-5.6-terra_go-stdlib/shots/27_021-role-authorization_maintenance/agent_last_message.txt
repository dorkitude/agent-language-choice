- Requires bearer auth; returns 401 if absent/invalid.
- Allows campaign owner or party member; otherwise 403.
- Returns `campaign_id`, `current_actor`, `phase`, and `turn_number`.
- Preserved existing play and legacy behavior; `run.sh` remains foreground launcher.
